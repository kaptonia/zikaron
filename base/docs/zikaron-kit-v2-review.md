# `zikaron.kit/2`: the review record

The record of the second client law's drafting, its rounds, and its naming.
The law is `docs/zikaron-kit-v2.md`; its base is the frozen `zikaron.kit/1`
(`docs/zikaron-kit-v1.md`, core `0x3f83…0535`, named 2026-09-05), which this
law leaves untouched; a `zikaron.kit/1` document is read by that law.

## Revision of 2026-10-27: packages, one-file kits, and the continued ledger

**Why the law moved.** With `zikaron/2` the client's reading law had to read
a ledger of two segments and a grant of either law; the judge's 存废调查 found
no consumer for fingerprint manifests, acknowledgements, depth readings and
proof files, and a consumer without a law for the one-file record kit, the
grant package (the judge's 2026-10-27 addendum: a package carries the
originals and travels encrypted, and one package both verifies the grant and
delivers the work), the directory digest convention, `anchoredOn`, `anchors`
(the plan's revision of 19:48) and the original names.

**What changed.** §1's `accept` dispatches by `spec` and `audit` yields the
ledger of both segments; §2 names the documents left; §3 is the badge (base
§6), reading `upstream` under either law; §4 is the record kit (base §7)
with the one-file form (§4.1a), the manifest's `name_md`, prefix rule, two
readings of a `contents` row, `anchoredOn` and `anchors` (§4.3), the
directory manifest recomputed at verification (§4.4 step 5, §4.5), and no
proofs; §5 is the grant package, its form, eight-step verdict and result
form; §6 is reachability through `continues` and the counted anchors of two
segments (base §8); §7 the grant check (base §10) with the root of a
continued ledger, timeliness in check 3 and coverage over both lineages; §8
the tokens, §9 the openings (encryption, retracted records), §10 the freeze,
unnamed, embedding the `zikaron/2` core.

**Where the drafting departed from the judge's plan**, for the judge to
confirm:

1. §4.1a names the subject of `E_KIT_UNREADABLE` exactly: the path of the
   item being read where its path line was read in full and is a kit path,
   and `.` otherwise; the plan said "the path of the item at which the
   departure was found, or `.` where none had been read".
2. §4.3's `members` rule reads "lacks one of the six required keys or
   carries a key beyond the eight"; `anchors` rows sort by `entry_id`
   bytewise, `chainId` numerically, `tx` bytewise.
3. §5.2's steps are numbered and each failure names its subject (the first
   uncarried hop in payload order, the first mismatching `contents` row, the
   bytewise-smallest unmatched terms path without its prefix); §5.3, the
   result form, is new, the plan having left `GP_OK`'s carried values to the
   harness.
4. §7.1 states the root a caller takes (the terms', else the grant's
   `ledger`, else its `author`) as caller guidance, the predicate reading
   the audit input's root as before.
5. HARNESS-KIT2.md: `kit-verify` and `grant-package` take a path that is a
   directory or a regular file, the file read as the one-file form.
